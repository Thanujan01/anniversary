✍️ The actual letter

To Thamil, on four years

It started at 10:44 at night on the twenty-third of October. A message. Nothing else.

Three weeks later I told you at 11:11 in the morning, and then I spent the whole day working myself up to ask you properly at 10:51 that night. You took two days. On the sixteenth of November you said yes, and that is the day everything on this website counts from.

Then 8:37 in the morning on the eighteenth — the first "love you", typed. Then 7:15 the next evening, out loud, in your actual voice. I wrote all of those times down. Most people keep the day. I wanted the minute.

We had our worst fight on the twenty-third of November, seven days in. One day without talking. It felt enormous and it is now one short line on a timeline four years long.

We numbered the first five meet-ups because they were rare enough to count — the twenty-first of November, then twenty days, then three whole months. After the fifth we stopped counting, which is its own kind of answer.

There are sixty-three photographs of us here. The oldest is a blue-lit evening on the twenty-sixth of October 2022, before any of it had a name. The newest is exactly four years later, both of us in helmets, going somewhere. In between: a field in October where we came back wearing rings, letters taller than we are, water to the horizon, a saree, balloons, salt in your hair. Almost none of them are special occasions. That is the part I like most.

You fed me before you ate. You stayed when it was not easy. You laughed at your own jokes before you finished them, every time, and I never once got tired of it.

So: one thousand four hundred and sixty-one days, and the number goes up again tomorrow.

I would choose you again. Every single time.

— Thanu 💞
